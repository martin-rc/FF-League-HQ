How good is the model?

Every game in weeks 3 to 8, predicted using only what was known before kickoff. Lower Brier and log loss are better. Thirty-six games is a small sample, so treat this as a sanity check.

Lineup value = what a team's best healthy lineup should score this week.

Every rostered player gets a value: his points a game in this league's scoring, weighted toward his last three games (65% season, 35% last 3; players with 1 or 2 games are marked down a little). Players who are Out, Doubtful, on IR, PUP or suspended are left out. Then we pick the best legal lineup (QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, 2 FLEX, K, DEF) from what is left. It reflects trades, pickups and injuries as of today. Bye weeks are not counted.

What is luck here?

Every week we pretend you played all 11 other teams. Beat 8 of them and that week is worth 8/11 = 0.73 "deserved" wins. Add up every week and you get the wins your scores earned. Luck = actual wins − deserved wins.

🍀 Lucky win: you won with a below-median score (your opponent laid an egg). 💀 Unlucky loss: you lost with an above-median score (you ran into a buzzsaw).

How start/sit is graded

Each week we rebuild the best legal lineup from the players on your roster (QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, 2 FLEX, K, DEF) using what they actually scored. It is the same "max points" number Sleeper shows, and ours matches it for every team.

🧠 Lineup IQ: your real points ÷ the best lineup's points. 🪑 Left on the bench: the difference. 😩 Avoidable loss: you lost, but your best lineup would have outscored your opponent.

Hindsight is perfect and Sunday mornings aren't, so 100% is out of reach for everyone. It still shows who leaves the most on the table.

How the numbers work
  • Records, points for and against are Sleeper's official numbers.
  • Tap any player's picture (or search in the top right) for his card. Pick a team under Viewing as to highlight it everywhere; the choice is remembered on this device only.
  • Playoff odds come from 20,000 simulated seasons over the real remaining schedule. Team value uses each rostered player's points a game in league scoring, weighted toward his last 3 games, with injured players left out.
  • Weekly scores come from Sleeper's matchup data. They were checked against the official results: every game's winner matches, and every team's weekly points add up to its official points for and against.
  • Player points use the league's own scoring settings, which reproduce all 1,628 player scores Sleeper shows for weeks 1 to 8.
  • The best lineup each week is rebuilt from the whole roster and matches Sleeper's max points for every team.
  • Draft value compares a pick's average points a game with a curve fitted to what each slot returns so far (3+ games).
  • Profile pictures are each manager's Sleeper avatar and player headshots come from Sleeper, saved Nov 4 2026.
